Ding Chuan

Media Professional

Ding Chuan is a media professional with over ten years of experience covering major international events, with a focus on tennis. He has reported on the 2017 and 2021 National Games, the 2023 Asian Games in Hangzhou, and the 2024 Paris Olympics, alongside tennis events including the 2017 Australian Open, 2018 Wimbledon, and the 2018 and 2019 WTA Finals. He holds a Master of Science in AI and Digital Media from Hong Kong Baptist University, and is now looking to leverage his reporting background in new directions spanning communications, media strategy, and beyond.
